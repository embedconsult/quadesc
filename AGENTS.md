# Repository guidance

## Product naming

- Always use the full product name **BeagleConnect QuadESC** in reader-facing content. Never shorten it to **QuadESC**.
- Never place an article (a, an or the) before **BeagleConnect QuadESC** or **BeagleConnect Torque**, including when the name modifies a noun such as board, carrier or manual. Rewrite the sentence if needed.
- Apply these rules to headings, prose, navigation, tables, diagram labels, accessible descriptions and product metadata.
- Preserve technical paths, URLs, filenames and identifiers such as `quadesc`; these are not product names.
